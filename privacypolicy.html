BTB - Beyond The Body: privacy

BTB is a personal fitness app. Everything you log (workouts, runs, body measurements, photos, settings) stays on your phone.

If you allow it, BTB reads your heart rate from Health Connect, only for the time of a run you have recorded, so it can show it next to that run. This data is stored on your phone with the run. It is never sent anywhere by BTB and is never written back to Health Connect.

If you use the optional AI coach with your own API key, a short summary of your training is sent to Anthropic to answer your question. You can remove the key at any time.

You can erase everything BTB has saved with "Erase everything and start fresh" in the Me tab.
